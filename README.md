### Hi, I'm Spencer 👋

High school student doing cancer research in the **Zhang Lab** at the
**University of Toledo** (Department of Cell and Cancer Biology). I'm interested
in how breast cancer spreads, and in the computational side of making sense of
tumor data: turning raw measurements into results a lab can rely on.

🌐 [spencerfrangou.github.io](https://spencerfrangou.github.io/) ·
🆔 [ORCID 0009-0008-1125-4053](https://orcid.org/0009-0008-1125-4053)

**What I'm working on**

- 🧬 Breast cancer metastasis and tumor transcriptomics
- 🔬 Targeted RNA panels and separating tumor signal from host signal in xenografts
- 🔭 Measuring how cancer cells grow and survive under different conditions
- 📊 Reproducible analysis pipelines in R and Python

**Research software I have built**

| | | |
|---|---|---|
| [**XenoPanel**](https://github.com/SpencerFrangou/XenoPanel) | Configuration-driven pipeline for targeted transcriptomic panels: species cross-reactivity masking in xenografts, edgeR differential expression, a heritable/environmental decomposition, and network entropy and pathway activity. R core, optional Python plotting layer. | [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23768220.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23768220) [![bio.tools](https://img.shields.io/badge/bio.tools-xenopanel-orange)](https://bio.tools/xenopanel) |
| [**ColonyCounter**](https://github.com/SpencerFrangou/ColonyCounter) | Quantification of colony formation (clonogenic) assays from flatbed scans of stained plates: well detection, optical-density colony segmentation with edge handling, a fillable plate map, design-aware statistics and publication figures. Python. | [![DOI](https://zenodo.org/badge/DOI/10.5281/zenodo.23780010.svg)](https://doi.org/10.5281/zenodo.23780010) [![bio.tools](https://img.shields.io/badge/bio.tools-colonycounter-orange)](https://bio.tools/colonycounter) |

Both are MIT-licensed, documented, tested on synthetic data in CI, and archived with citable DOIs.
Issues and questions are welcome on either repository.
